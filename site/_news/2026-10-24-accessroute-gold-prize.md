🏆 Our project [**AccessRoute HK**](https://xanderzhou2022.github.io/AccessRoute/) reached the finals and won **Gold Prize (Winner)** in **Track 1: Green Transport and Smart Mobility** at the [Hong Kong Inter-University GenAI Hackathon for Sustainable Development Goals 2026](https://www.hack4sdg.com/) on **October 24, 2026**!

AccessRoute supports accessible first- and last-mile journeys in Hong Kong, combining floor-level maps and mobility-aware route planning with AI-assisted preference input, visual localization, obstacle reporting, and Cantonese guidance.

Congratulations to our team, **HumanRoute (Team 1022)**, and thank you to the organisers and everyone who supported the project!
