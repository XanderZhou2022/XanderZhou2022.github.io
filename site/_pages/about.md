I am **Yixi (Xander) Zhou**, a Ph.D. student in computer science at [Hong Kong Baptist University](https://www.hkbu.edu.hk/), advised by Prof. [Renchi Yang](https://renchi.ac.cn/) in the [LAGAS Group](https://renchi.ac.cn/team/). I received my B.S. from ShanghaiTech University and was an exchange student at the University of Wisconsin–Madison.

My research focuses on **data agents and reliable LLM-based systems** for complex, real-world data. I am interested in **retrieval, structured reasoning, and trustworthy evaluation** for data management and analytics.

I also work on **financial AI** with collaborators at [MBZUAI](https://mbzuai.ac.ae/) and [The University of Tokyo](https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/). My recent work spans financial document QA, Text-to-SQL evaluation, and adaptive data discovery.
