_Why Does TikTok Know What You Like?_ is an interactive lesson created for **HKBU MCCP6010 · Group 6**. It introduces recommendation systems through familiar video-feed examples and a four-step journey: **collect signals, understand content, rank videos, and update recommendations**.

## What It Covers

- **User signals:** Compare explicit feedback, such as likes and follows, with viewing behavior, such as watch time, skips, and rewatches.
- **Content clues:** Explore how topics, captions, hashtags, sounds, and shared interests can help identify candidate videos.
- **Ranking and feedback:** Calculate scores using a simplified classroom rule, explain a ranking, and see how new feedback can change the order.
- **Exploration and feed controls:** Discuss how repeated recommendations may narrow exposure and how viewers can influence their feeds.

## Interactive Features

The lesson combines step-by-step reveals, worked examples, pair discussions, timed activities, and a closing three-question quiz. Presentation tools include keyboard navigation, a page overview, speaker notes, fullscreen mode, and print-to-PDF support. The closing quiz supports configurable Kahoot links and an on-screen fallback.

The feeds, scores, and updates are classroom illustrations. The ranking rule is a teaching model rather than TikTok's disclosed algorithm.

## Tech Stack

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, SVG illustrations, and browser-based presentation tools.
